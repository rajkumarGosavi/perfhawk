# Code of conduct

Perfhawk follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
By taking part in this project you agree to abide by it.

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, give and accept feedback graciously, and do not harass anyone.

## Reporting

To report unacceptable behaviour, use GitHub's private
["Report a vulnerability"](https://github.com/rajkumarGosavi/perfhawk/security/advisories/new) form and say it is a
conduct report, or contact the maintainer through the email on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributors that violate this code.
